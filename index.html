Professional Summary

I'm an enthusiastic IT undergraduate pursuing a Bachelor of Information and Communication Technology Honours [BICT (Hons)] at the University of Vavuniya, Faculty of Technological Studies. I'm passionate about building innovative software solutions across multiple domains.
